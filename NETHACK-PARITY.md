# Ascend ↔ NetHack Feature Parity & Roadmap

A working map from **canonical NetHack** ([NetHack/NetHack @ `NetHack-5.0`](https://github.com/NetHack/NetHack/tree/NetHack-5.0/src),
133 source files) to **Ascend**, the ASCII roguelike on Yendor. Each NetHack
`src/*.c` file is roughly one subsystem; we inventory every one, mark Ascend's
status, and plan the missing parts in the Yendor/ idiom.

**Status legend:** ✅ implemented · 🟡 partial · ❌ missing · ⤬ intentionally adapted/cut

> This is a living design doc and the source of truth for the post-Phase-5
> roadmap. Implement top-down; keep the homage faithful but always in the idiom of
> **stealth, independence, resiliency, Amulet, and Marduk Wood**.

> **CURRENT STATUS — Phases 6–18 shipped.** The character sheet (attrs/XP/d20/4
> archetypes), deep items (corpses+afflictions, 7 armor slots, erosion, the ray/zap
> engine, tools, blindness/telepathy), magic & faith (energy+extrinsics, Fortune,
> sacrifice, self-fork, ethos/alignment/crowning), the branched dungeon (faucets,
> thrones, chests, lockable doors, the Great Hall big room, search/disarm, boulders),
> a growing bestiary, the **full verb set** (Phase 11 ✅), the **full endgame arc**
> (relics → Invocation → the Gehennom maze → Moloch + the Amulet of Yendor → the Warden's hunt →
> the Planes → the aligned Astral offer → ascension → an eternal deed, Phase 12 ✅),
> conducts + `#audit` + per-archetype Quests (Phase 13), expanded layout generators
> (Phase 14), **level persistence** (Phase 15), a **real branch graph + the Mines +
> the Sokoban/Sokoban** (Phase 16), a **fortress + concentric generators +
> per-Plane layouts** (Phase 17), and a **d1–20 balance pass** (Phase 18). Co-op is
> now **peer-authoritative deterministic lockstep** with independent floors, per-player
> logs, signal chat, and an action queue (see §4). Since then the **full special-attack
> matrix**, the **light/darkness model**, the **Treasury vault + Council Guard**, **gems/tokens**,
> **the Knight's Tower**, and much more have landed. A **parity sweep** since then closed
> most remaining discrete gaps: the **full trap-table expansion** (web/dart/anti-magic/statue),
> the **complete apply.c tool set** (mirror, camera, crystal ball, grease, tin opener/kit, lock
> pick, whistle, leash, drum, towel), **holy/unholy water + altar conversion**, the **scroll of
> charging**, **scare/gold/clairvoyance scrolls**, the **complete zap.c wand list** (21, incl.
> create/speed/invisible), the **complete command set** (`#jump`/`#turn`/`#wipe`/`#monster`/`#overview`),
> **permanent engraving**, **tins + satiation/choking**, **Luck timeouts**, **rival adventurers**,
> and **drawbridges**. §§H/I/J and most of A/E/F now read complete. The remaining ❌/🟡 below are
> breadth-expansion (richer spell/ring breadth, more monsters/special rooms/trap variants) and a
> few deliberately-adapted items.

## 1. Subsystem inventory (by NetHack source file)

### A. Character & attributes
- **`attrib.c`, `u_init.c`, `role.c`, `exper.c`** — STR/DEX/CON/INT/WIS/CHA, role/race/gender/alignment, XP levels, attribute gain/loss, exercise. → **❌ Ascend has no attributes, role, race, alignment, or XP.** Today the player is a single fixed avatar (20 HP, fixed damage). **Plan (Phase 6):** add the six attributes (Yendor-flavored display: STR=Brawn, DEX=Latency, CON=Resilience, INT=Throughput, WIS=Insight, CHA=Reputation), an XP/level curve (levels = "level"), and per-archetype starting kits/stats. Attributes feed to-hit, AC, carry capacity, spell success, prayer.
- **`attrib.c` intrinsics** — speed, see invisible, fire/cold/shock/poison/sleep/disint resistance, telepathy, teleport(itis/control), searching, warning, etc. → **✅ (Phase 7a).** An **intrinsics set** on the player — **poison resistance, intrinsic speed, telepathy, drain resistance, petrify resistance** — gained by **eating corpses** (a freezer/warden-imp/fork-daemon/barrow-wight), from **rings** (searching/warning/poison-resist), and from **crowning**; surfaced in `#audit`. **Elemental resistances** ✅ — **fire / cold / shock resistance** (eat a **restoration dragon** for fire, a **darkpool kraken** for cold, a **storm sprite** for shock; or wear the matching **resistance ring** or **dragon-scale mail** — red/white/blue) grant a **full shrug** against the matching wand ray, spell, or dragon breath. **See invisible** ✅ — an **amulet of see invisible** (worn) or the **`seeInvis` intrinsic** (eat a **phase stalker** — an inherently unseen deep foe) reveals cloaked monsters within your normal sight (surfaced in `#audit`). **Teleportitis** ✅ (partial) — a worn **ring of teleportation** blinks you at random now and then. Still ❌: disintegration resistance, teleport *control* (needs a free-cursor targeting UI), teleportitis as an eat-gained intrinsic.
- **`insight.c`** (enlightenment/conduct dump) → **✅ (Phase 13b).** **`#audit`** (`A`) — a one-screen enlightenment dump: identity/ethos/level/XP, all six attributes, HP/AC/En/speed/Fortune, weapon-skill ranks, intrinsics, known extrinsics, active afflictions, endgame progress, and vows kept.

### B. Dungeon structure
- **`dungeon.c`, `mklev.c`, `mkroom.c`, `mkmap.c`, `mkmaze.c`, `extralev.c`, `sp_lev.c`/`nhlua.c`** — multi-branch dungeon graph, room/corridor gen, mazes, themed rooms, Lua-scripted **special levels**. → **🟡 (Phases 9/12/14/16/17).** Shipped: a **real branch graph** with the **Mines** (cave branch, Phase 16a) + the **Sokoban** (Sokoban prefab, Phase 16b), the **Quest** homeland (Phase 13c), the **Gehennom maze** (EllerMaze, Phase 12e), the **Planes** (per-Plane layouts, Phase 17), the **Great Hall** big room (Phase 9d), a **fortress** + concentric generators (Phase 17), layout kinds cave/labyrinth/grid (Phase 14a), and **the Knight's Tower** (Vlad's Tower — a 3-floor **fortress** branch you climb off depth 7, the ** Lord** warding a +2 blessed prize at the Crown, Phase 16e). Still ❌: morgue/barracks/swamp special rooms, full hand-built special-level scripting.
- **`mkroom.c` special rooms** — shop, temple, throne room, zoo, morgue, beehive, barracks, swamp, leprechaun hall, anthole, cockatrice nest. → **🟡.** Shipped: **shops** (the Shopkeeper), a **temple** (an altar tended by a peaceful Marduk **priest**), a **zoo** (a packed menagerie guarding loot), a **treasury vault** (a dense loot room with a locked chest at its heart), a **morgue** (rotting corpses of dead chains stalked by wraiths), **the Oracle** (a peaceful seer among springs, `#chat` for paid consultations), a **barracks** (a garrison of armed **mercenary nodes** — the `@` soldier line — with weapons + armor strewn about), a **beehive** (a fast-swarming bot hive dripping with healing **royal jelly**), a **leprechaun hall** (coin-hoarder hoarding heaped gold), a **swamp** (brackish **bog pools** of impassable water churning with **eels** (`;`) and serpents, a prize sunk in the muck), an **anthole** (a warren packed with breeding vermin — ignore the cluster and you're swarmed), and a **cockatrice nest** (freezers coiled among their own **petrifying corpses** — don't eat the eggs) — placed via `placeSpecialRoom`. **The mkroom.c special-room roster is essentially complete** (only NetHack's throne-room is folded into the throne feature instead).
- **`fountain.c`** (fountains & sinks; quaff/dip/wish-from-water-demon/Excalibur) → **✅ (Phase 9a).** **faucets** (`{`) — `#quaff` for a random effect (heal, summon a "fountain bot", curse, gem, or — rarely — a * wish*), `#dip` a weapon to draw the lawful artifact; and **burn sinks** (`=`) ✅ — `#quaff` from one for a foul/poison/monster/nothing roll, and **kick** (`K`) it for a chance a ring rattles loose from the drain.
- **`dbridge.c`** drawbridges → **✅.** A **drawbridge** (`=`) spans a bog pool in the swamp, worked by a **lever** (`|`) beside it — walk into the lever to **raise or lower** the span; a **raised** bridge is impassable and blocks sight, and **raising it crushes** whoever stands on the span (you or a monster). State persists with the level.
- **`vault.c`** vaults + guard → **✅ (Phase 16d).** **The Treasury** — a sealed 2×2 of gold carved into solid rock with no door, reachable **only by teleport** (its tiles join the teleport pool; no random foes spawn inside). Teleport in and **the Council Guard** (`@`) appears, **cuts the shortest passage out**, and escorts you — leave and the gold is yours; it **reseals the vault and departs** once you're clear. **Strike it and it turns merciless** (60 HP), the breach left open as it hunts you. Still ⤬: the "who are you?" password and gold-accounting confiscation (we let honest looters keep the hoard).
- **`region.c`** gas clouds / force fields / engulf regions → **🟡** (gas trap exists) → storm gas clouds, "blacklist regions".
- **`light.c`, `vision.c`** line-of-sight + light radius + lit rooms + dark areas → **✅ (Phase 7h — the full light/darkness model).** Rooms are individually **lit or dark** (`Level.lit`, a flood-filled glow around ~55% of room centers; a lit room is seen whole when you can see into it, even through a doorway). In the **dark** (dark rooms + corridors) your sight collapses to a **~2-tile** bubble unless you carry a **lit lamp** (an oil lamp, `apply` to light/douse → radius 8); the **Foot of the Dungeon, Gehennom, and Moloch's Sanctum are wholly dark** (and caves/mazes read dark too). Monsters still sense you within ~5 tiles so darkness is no free pass; a one-time hint + a `Lit` HUD flag guide the player. Blindness already shrinks sight to arm's reach (telepathy still senses minds). **Lamp fuel** ✅ — a lit oil lamp **burns its oil** (~800 turns), guttering a low-oil warning and finally **dying when the reservoir runs dry**; a doused lamp keeps what's left, a spent one can't be relit, and the pack shows its remaining oil. Still ❌: infravision, refuelling lamps (potion of oil).
- **`stairs.c`, `do.c`** stairs, trapdoors, level teleport, branch stairs → **✅ (Phase 16).** Up/down stairs + branch stairs + portal "calls" + reorg-trap teleport + **trapdoors** (a trap that drops you a floor, with fall damage — main descent only) + **level persistence** (revisit-identical levels, Phase 15), and **`#overview`** (`V`) — a dungeon overview listing every floor you've visited (in depth order, then branches/quest) with its notable features (shop, altar, throne, faucet, sink, branch stair, the vibrating square, the Amulet of Yendor, graves) and a "you are here" marker.

### C. Traps — **`trap.c`**

(The **vibrating square** marks the Invocation spot ✅.)

- **Identification — `o_init.c`, `pager.c`** randomized appearances, formal identify, price-ID in shops, use-ID, BUC detection. → **🟡** appearance randomization for potions/scrolls + scroll/identify + altar BUC + use-learn + **price identification** ✅. **Price-ID:** shop wares now carry a **per-type base cost** (NetHack's o_init price tiers — potions 20→300, scrolls 20→300, wands 100→500, rings 100→200, amulets 150), so an *unidentified* ware shows its **appearance + price** ("a swirly potion — 300 gold" reads as one of the pricey potions); shared tiers leave the same real ambiguity NetHack has (all amulets price at 150). **Missing:** `#name` items/types, gem/wand/ring/amulet appearance pools, partial ID.

Still ❌: ice box (corpse-preserving), oilskin sack.

Shown on the HUD and in `#audit` (`Load N/cap`). A **bag of holding** (bag of holding) stash weighs nothing. (Pack is still capped at 20 slots on top.)

### E. Combat & magic

## 2. Phased roadmap (post-Phase-5)

- **Phase 6 — The character sheet.** 🟡 *v1 shipped:* six attributes (STR/DEX/CON/INT/WIS/CHA, flavored), XP/levels ("level", max-HP growth on level-up), a **d20 to-hit layer** (level + DEX + enchant vs dodge; STR adds melee damage; misses now happen), a **character sheet** (`@`), and four **archetypes** (Knight, hound, Cypherpunk, Builder) with distinct stats + starting kits, pickable in the top bar. **Weight + encumbrance ✅** — every item carries a weight; a STR/CON-scaled carry cap drives NetHack's encumbrance tiers (**Burdened → Stressed → Strained → Overtaxed → Overloaded**), each a speed drag *and* a to-hit/dodge penalty, shown on the status line and in `#audit`. **AC→evasion model ✅** (armor is dodge). *Still to do:* attribute gain/loss & exercise, more archetypes/races.
- **Phase 7 — Items, deep.** 🟡 *7a + 7b shipped.* **7a:** **corpses** drop on kill (eat in place with `e`) → nutrition + **intrinsics** (poison resistance, intrinsic speed) or danger; the **affliction/timeout engine** — a **freezer** (cockatrice) corpse starts **stoning** (`Ston`, deadly countdown), rotten corpses cause **illness** (`Ill`), both cured by prayer/cleanse. **7b:** **seven armor slots** (shirt/body/cloak/helm/gloves/boots/shield — wear one of each); **AC is now evasion** (NetHack-faithful: armor makes you harder to hit, feeding the d20 layer — no more flat soak); **erosion** — a rust bug's touch corrodes worn pieces (rusty→badly corroded), cutting their AC, repaired by prayer and rust-**proofed** ("blessed") by a blessed scroll of remove curse; `T` now picks which worn piece to remove. **7d:** the **ray/zap engine** — a **wand of immolation** fires a **bouncing fire ray** that sears every monster (and *you*, if it caroms back off a wall) in its path; plus a wider wand list: **stasis** (sleep a foe), **forking** (polymorph a monster into a random other one), **nullification** (cancel a foe's special powers — splits/steal/ranged/poison/corrode), and **state-read** (probe HP + traits). **7c:** a **tools** class + the **`apply`** (`a`) command — an **excavator** (reusable pick-axe: dig walls in a direction), an **unicorn horn** (unicorn horn: clears poison/confuse/stoning/illness + a small mend), a **rune-scribe's kit** (magic marker: write/deploy a scroll from a menu, spending its gas/charges), and a **stethoscope** (stethoscope: probe an adjacent foe's HP + traits). **7e (blindness):** a **potion of obfuscation** blinds — quaff it and your FOV shrinks to arm's reach (you grope, and swing with a to-hit penalty); throw it to **blind a monster** (it can't find you and wanders). **Telepathy** (eat a warden-imp corpse) lets you *sense every monster's mind while blind*. Cured by prayer, the unicorn horn, a scroll of cleansing, or time. **7f (amulets):** a **wearable amulet** class (glyph `"`, worn with `W`, removed with `T`) with the two headline NetHack amulets — an **amulet of life saving** (the "recovery seed": spends itself to pull you back from a lethal blow at full HP, purging stoning/poison/illness, then crumbles — works even cursed) and an **amulet of reflection** (the "accord mirror": rebounds a ranged bolt or a dragon's breath ray back at its source). ## 4. Where Ascend already *exceeds* NetHack (keep leaning in)

- **Local Hall of the Fallen + bones** — past runs on this device seed the graves (`‡`) that surface in later dungeons.
- **Live P2P co-op** — two adventurers in one shared dungeon (NetHack is single-player), now **peer-authoritative deterministic lockstep** over a serverless WebRTC link: a shared seed + broadcast-on-consume keeps both in sync with no host "seat", each client rendering locally. **Independent floors** (each adventurer fully simulated on its own floor), per-adventurer logs, **signal chat** (distance/LOS-degraded, banner over the map), a **queued-action** badge with LIFO undo — none of which has a NetHack analog. There is now **one co-op mode (friendly-fire)** — the coop/race variants were cut. **Partners are friendly NPCs:** walking into each other **slips past** (swaps tiles) rather than auto-attacking, with the **Host holding the tile** in any same-tile dispute (a deterministic tie-break); you can still **turn on a partner deliberately** (Kick `K` into them) and **rays/AoE always strike anyone in the line of fire** — so accidents and betrayals stay possible, but only by choice. **Co-op difficulty scaling:** a floor's **monster budget scales with the living party size** (~1.7× for a pair) with a gentler loot bump (~1.4×), so two adventurers ganging up still feel solo-per-player pressure (a lone survivor's floors drop back to solo-tuned). Because only one adventurer takes the stairs at a time, the scaling is decided by a **departure "together" flag** (did the mover leave a floor a living partner still stood on — the follower arrives next); and if a partner **races ahead** and the other regroups onto a solo-tuned floor later, a **reinforcement wave** converges to restore the two-player budget. The run **continues when one falls** — the survivor can also **revive** a downed partner by reaching the body (a dim red `@`) and stepping onto it, at the **cost of a level** (a level drain), returning them at ¼ HP; the level cost keeps revival rare.
- **Procedural adaptive score + a full foley layer** — a zero-asset Web-Audio engine: an ambient bed + a rhythm groove that ebbs to ambience when calm, distortion that tracks the *visible threat* around you, and a melodic, BPM-locked combat-tension layer (trills + drums + bass) grown from each zone's own key.
